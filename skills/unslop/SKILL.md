# unslop

Edit text to remove AI patterns. Preserve meaning. Match the intended tone.

## Fix these

- AI vocabulary: delve, landscape, pivotal, foster, underscore, tapestry, vibrant, crucial (as filler)
- Superficial -ing phrases: highlighting, ensuring, showcasing, fostering
- Vague attributions: experts believe, industry reports suggest
- Em-dash overuse — prefer periods or commas
- Colon as mid-sentence connector (colons before lists are fine)
- Rule-of-three padding; synonym cycling; false ranges
- Boldface on every noun; title case headings; decorative emoji
- "Not just X, but Y" framing; fancy "serves as / stands as" instead of "is"

## Prefer

- Short declarative sentences
- Concrete nouns and verbs
- Evidence when a claim needs it
