# Babysit

Drive a PR or stack to merge-ready: conflicts, review threads, CI.

1. Resolve forge (`gh` preferred).
2. List blockers: CI, conflicts, review comments, required checks.
3. Fix real blockers with evidence. Dismiss noise with a concrete reason.
4. Do not land until independently verified against acceptance criteria.
5. Declare mode (watch vs drive) when polling long-running CI.
6. **Reply:** status, remaining blockers, what you fixed, what you dismissed.
