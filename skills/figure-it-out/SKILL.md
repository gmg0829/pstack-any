# figure-it-out

1. Restate the goal and how you will know it is done.
2. Design a **short** sequence of verifiable units (not a novel process religion).
3. Put the sequence on a worklist.
4. Execute with evidence. Adjust only when evidence demands it.
5. **Reply:** custom sequence, results, proof of done.
